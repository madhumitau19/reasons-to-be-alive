Reasons to be Alive

Reasons to be Alive is a collection of personal motivations and fascinations that different individuals have that they believe is a reason to continue living and find joy in being alive. These could be the littlest things or the larger things in life, but the purpose is to find that everyone can find some reason to be alive.
